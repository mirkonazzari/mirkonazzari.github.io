I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Political Science at the [Department of Law of the University of Sassari](https://giurisprudenza.uniss.it/), where I am Principal Investigator of the project **SCUDO**, the first impact evaluation of the Italian national fund supporting municipalities whose local administrators have been targeted by threats and intimidation.

I hold an International PhD in Criminology from [Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore](https://www.unicatt.it/) in Milan, where I also worked for more than three years as a researcher at [Transcrime](https://www.transcrime.it/en/), the Joint Research Centre on Innovation and Crime. During my PhD, I was a visiting student at the Department of Criminology and Criminal Justice of the **University of Maryland**.

My research focuses on money laundering and its control, organized and financial crime, cybercrime and cryptocurrencies, and the evaluation of urban security and crime-prevention policies. Methodologically, I rely on quantitative and causal-inference designs (difference-in-differences, panel data, cross-national longitudinal analysis), social network analysis, and original data collection from administrative and open sources.

I am a founder of the Money Laundering Research Network (MLRN) and a member of the Editorial Board of [*Evidence Base: Criminal Justice Research, Policy & Action*](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rebj20).
