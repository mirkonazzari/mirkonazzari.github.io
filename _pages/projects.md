## SCUDO – Principal Investigator

*University of Sassari, April 2026 – present*

**S**trumenti di policy per il **C**ontrasto alla violenza politica e la t**U**tela della **D**emocrazia l**O**cale. The first impact evaluation of the Italian *Fondo per la legalità e la tutela degli amministratori locali vittime di atti intimidatori*, the national fund for municipalities whose local administrators have been targeted by threats and intimidation.

- Building an original municipality–month panel database (about 7,900 Italian municipalities, 2015–2025) that integrates Ministry of the Interior allocation data, coded municipal council resolutions, intimidation episodes (Avviso Pubblico, ACLED) and socio-economic controls (ISTAT, OpenBilanci).
- Estimating the causal effect of the Fund with a generalised difference-in-differences design and estimators robust to staggered treatment adoption, with a focus on Sardinia and its pre-existing regional legislation (L.R. 21/1998).
